01 / THE PERSON

Hi, I'm Helena ✿

I'm a Computer Science student at Columbia Engineering, interested in how technology and design can shape thoughtful, human-centered experiences.

In this class, I want to learn how to turn an idea into a physical, interactive system. I'm excited to experiment with sensors, microcontrollers, and everyday materials, then document what works, what doesn't, and what I learn along the way.

✳
